## Findings
- NeetCode-150-style two-pointer group (per a list summary in the search result): Valid Palindrome, Two Sum II, 3Sum, Container With Most Water, Trapping Rain Water. Blind 75 reportedly has 3 two-pointer problems (3Sum, Container With Most Water, and one more; exact list not verified). A broader two-pointer list in the same result also names 3Sum Closest, Reverse String, Valid Palindrome II, 3Sum Smaller, Subarray Product Less Than K, 4Sum, Backspace String Compare, Is Subsequence.
- Google-tagged two-pointer list (hackmnc.com, "frequency" percentages, method not explained): Trapping Rain Water 85.9%, Merge Sorted Array 81.4%, Longest Palindromic Substring 77.1%, 3Sum 76.7%, Container With Most Water 75.1%, Merge Strings Alternately 75.0%, Next Permutation 63.2%, Remove Duplicates from Sorted Array 62.9%, Reverse Words in a String 59.5%, Valid Palindrome 59.3%.
- Cross-company counts (same summary): Trapping Rain Water 100 appearances across 42 companies, Longest Palindromic Substring 89, 3Sum 85, Merge Sorted Array 65, Container With Most Water 62.
- Amazon: two pointers is the 6th most common pattern, 15.6% of 78 questions (dsaprep.dev, method unknown).
- Local cache (`dsa-company-interview-calibration-2026.md`): 680 Valid Palindrome II is among the most-reported Meta E4 problems. Local cache (`dsa-array-problem-frequency-2026.md`): 3Sum, Container With Most Water, Trapping Rain Water, Move Zeroes recur across lists.

## Reliability
Third-party prep sites; percentages are unexplained crowd data. Treat as a signal. Longest Palindromic Substring is usually solved with expand-around-centre (a two-pointer idea) but is a string/DP problem; not solved in the notes.
