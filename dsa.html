MethodHow each topic is taught

Every topic follows the same 15 parts, in the same order: first principles, language toolkit, complexity, core patterns, pattern recognition, brute force to optimal, representative problems, problem-solving method, edge cases, common mistakes, templates, mental model, mastery checklist, final practice and a practical build track. The master prompt is dsa/das_prompt.md.

Notes for Binary Search and the later topics are generated from that prompt, one topic at a time.
